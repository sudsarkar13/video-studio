# Third-party components

Video Studio does not bundle these. The installer fetches them, or they are already on your system.

| Component | Used for | Licence | Fetched from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kokoro-82M voice model (`kokoro-v1.0.onnx`, `voices-v1.0.bin`) | Narration | Apache-2.0 (model by hexgrad) | GitHub release of kokoro-onnx, checksum-verified |
| kokoro-onnx | Running the voice model | MIT | PyPI |
| onnxruntime | Model inference | MIT | PyPI (dependency of kokoro-onnx) |
| soundfile, numpy | Audio files, music and effects synthesis | BSD-3-Clause | PyPI |
| playwright-core | Driving the browser to record and render | Apache-2.0 | npm |
| ffmpeg | Encoding, mixing, loudness | LGPL/GPL (your system build) | Your package manager |
| Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge / Chromium | Recording and rendering | Their own licences | Already installed, or Playwright's Chromium download |

The background music and sound effects are generated by `scripts/sound.py` at render time. They contain no samples, so no licence restrictions attach to them.

Check the voice model's licence at its source before commercial use if your situation is unusual. Any music file you supply yourself must be licensed for your use.
